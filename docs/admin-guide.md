# EMC Portal: admin how-to

Open **https://idrisadeyemi01.github.io/emc/** on your phone. Tip: in Chrome, tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen.

## Send today's reminders

1. Tap **Teacher Reminders**. Today's teachers load straight from the `Teachers' Contacts` sheet.
2. On each card, tap **Send on WhatsApp**. WhatsApp opens in that teacher's chat with the message filled in; tap send there.
3. Come back to the site. The card now shows **Sent ✓**, and the counter at the top goes up ("4 of 7 sent").
4. When every card is sent, the page says so.

## Other things you can do

- **Remind the night before:** tap tomorrow's day chip at the top. The message says "at EMC on Saturday" instead of "today".
- **Change a message before sending:** tap **Show message** and edit it. Edits aren't saved to the sheet.
- **Copy instead:** **Copy message**, then paste it anywhere.
- **Undo a mark:** on a sent card, tap **Mark as not sent**.
- **Sheet changed?** Tap **Refresh** (or reload the page). The site always shows what's in the sheet now.

## Warnings on a card

- **Invalid phone:** the number in the sheet isn't a Nigerian mobile number (11 digits starting with 0, or 13 starting with 234). Fix it in the sheet and refresh. Or copy the message, send it another way, and tap **Mark as sent**.
- **Start time missing:** the sheet has no start time for that class. Type it after `*Start Time* :` in the message. Send stays off until you do. Better: fill it in the sheet.
- **"1 row in the sheet couldn't be read":** a row is missing its day, class, subject, teacher or phone, or the day is misspelled. Fix that row in the sheet.

## Good to know

- Sent marks are saved on **this phone's browser** only. Another phone or laptop starts with nothing marked.
- To change who gets reminders, edit the `Teachers' Contacts` tab: add, remove or change rows. Nothing else needs updating.
